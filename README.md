## 📌 Executive Summary

**Aether** is an AI-powered smart city exploration, safety, and civic triage platform built for **40+ major Indian Tier-1, Tier-2, and state capital cities** (including Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune, Solapur, Jaipur, and Kochi).

Navigating urban environments across India presents distinct challenges—ranging from poorly lit corridors and unpredictable festival crowd congestion to vernacular barriers during civic reporting. **Aether** transforms urban navigation through a **zero-paid-keys architecture**, bringing together:

* **Safety-Weighted $A^*$ Routing:** Dynamic pathfinding with real-time festival crowd avoidance.
* **Dual-Layer Trust Engine:** Separates general tourist feedback from verified resident reviews.
* **6-Category Heritage Directory:** Cultural landmark discovery with native regional RAG audio guides.
* **Vernacular Citizen Triage:** Multimodal hazard reporting backed by DBSCAN spatial clustering.
* **Offline Resilience Protocol:** Emergency SMS payload generation and offline-scannable QR cards.
* **Global 10-Language Vernacular Support:** Full UI localization across English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Kannada, Gujarati, Malayalam, and Punjabi.
